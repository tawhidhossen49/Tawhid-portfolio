Amaze Youth Chess Tournament

The official home of a national youth chess championship — format, registration, a live countdown and a player portal.

What it is

The Amaze Youth Chess Tournament is an annual championship for Bangladeshi students under 30. The site explains the whole format — an open Selection Round on Lichess, eight groups of four and a knockout road to the final — takes registrations, counts down to the first move, and leads players into a portal where every game is played live.

My role

Design and development, end to end: the chrome-on-black visual system, scroll-driven motion with GSAP and Lenis, the build, and deployment on Vercel.
