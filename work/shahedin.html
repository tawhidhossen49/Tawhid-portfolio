What it is

Shahedin is a concept project: a Bangla-language platform built around a YouTube educator who teaches geopolitics, history, economics and soft skills. The demo brings together featured videos, paid courses, a product store and the educator’s story, with sign-in and a member dashboard backed by Supabase.

My role

Design and development, end to end: a Bangla-first layout and type, course and store pages, Supabase-backed accounts and content, and deployment on Vercel.
