What it is

Amaze Consortium is a youth-led, multi-sector organisation that brings independently run subsidiaries under one strategic vision. The site introduces the consortium and how it is structured, its subsidiaries, founder, executive members and advisors, and the ways to get involved — internships, donations and partnerships.

My role

Design and full-stack development, end to end: a bold black-and-paper type system, scroll motion with GSAP and Lenis, the build, and deployment on Vercel.
